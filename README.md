#  The Atrium

> *"A liminal, eerie, and oddly peaceful mystery where nothing is quite as it seems"*

**The Atrium** is an interactive, slide-based murder mystery adventure game built entirely in **Microsoft PowerPoint**. Combining pixel art aesthetics with atmospheric, distorted environmental design, players take on the role of an detective searching for clues, confronting suspects, and unraveling an unsettling truth trapped within a surreal space.

## Lore & Synopsis

You wake up inside **The Atrium**, a sprawling, multi-story structure bathed in an oddly soothing yet deeply unsettling atmosphere. A crime has been committed, but the boundaries of reality feel blurred.

### **Act I: The First Floor & Isolation**

As you explore the first floor, you encounter the first two suspects. Navigating through quiet, dreamlike rooms, you collect preliminary evidence and escort them into the **Holding Room** on the upper level, believing you have the situation under control.

### **Act II: The Escalation & The Second Floor**

All of a sudden, you pass out. And everything… restarted..?

Upon reaching the second floor, the tension erupts. Drama unfolds inside the Holding Room as the suspects turn on each other. You quickly realize your initial evidence is insufficient to prove anything. To uncover the whole story, you continue venturing deeper and encountering anomalies on the way.

> **The Reality:** As the investigation deepens, the line between waking life and dream begins to fracture. Clues slip away, the structure shifts, and discovering the truth may reveal a much darker loop...

## Gameplay Features

* **Point-and-Click Slide Navigation:** Fully mapped interactive triggers, custom action buttons, and branching room paths built inside PowerPoint.

* **Pixel Art Aesthetics:** Retro, pixelated visuals designed to enhance the distorted, liminal, and dreamlike atmosphere.

* **Multi-Floor Exploration:** Explore complex layouts across two distinct floors, using holding rooms, and environmental puzzles.

* **Evidence & Suspect Management:** Collect clues, unlock dialogue branches, and question suspects.

## Built With

* **Engine:** Microsoft PowerPoint (Hyperlinks, Animations, & Transitions)

* **Art Style:** Pixel Art Assets & Custom Scene Maps

* **Platform:** Interactive Presentation / Slide Deck

## How to Play

1. Download `The_Atrium.pptx` from the repository releases.

2. Open the file in **Microsoft PowerPoint**.

3. Start the presentation in **Slide Show Mode** (`F5`).

4. Use your mouse to click on interactive object triggers, navigate through doors, examine clues, and interview suspects.
